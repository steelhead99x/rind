# [XL] RSA signing, decryption, and key generation are proven

## Goal

RSA's private operation and everything around it, PKCS#1 v1.5 and PSS signing, OAEP encryption and decryption, key loading, and key generation, are proven from the API to the Montgomery products and table gathers, so RSA-2048 signing earns the ✓ on x86_64 with BMI2 and ADX, with AVX-512 IFMA too, and on every path whose products a later quest proves.

## Plan

- Proven: `proofs/Rind/X86_64/RsaPrivate.lean`'s `private_op_spec` takes `RsaPrivateKey::private_op` down to s2n-bignum's products, through the CRT, `PosInt::mod_exp_pair`, the Montgomery conversions, `mont_exp`'s 5-bit fixed window (`RsaExp.lean`), and its table gather on both paths (`RsaGather.lean`, over `GatherModel.lean`'s AVX2 model), and with AVX-512 IFMA through pyca/verified-garbage's CRT (`crt_ifma_spec`), which composes `VgRsa.lean`'s axiom `ifma_crt_spec`. It holds for a key meeting `PrivKeyInv` on BMI2 and ADX; where VG's CRT does not run (`key.ifma.w = 0`), with primes of 16 or 32 words where the dual RSAZ does not run either (`NoRsazPair`). `private_op_rsa2048_spec` discharges the products and the dual RSAZ for RSA-2048. `RsaPrivKey.lean`'s `rsa_private_key_new_spec` proves that a key `RsaPrivateKey::new` accepts meets `PrivKeyInv`, VG's CRT values included (`IfmaCrtInv`, from `IfmaCrt::precompute` against `VG.Spec.Rsa.privatePrecompute`), holds the components it was given, and that they meet RFC 8017's relations (`CrtRelations`), given that `HaveAvx512ForRsa`'s CPU checks return and `MontProducts` at the wider prime's width. It characterizes the keys `new` returns, not which inputs it accepts: that it accepts the keys it supports is tested, not proven.
- Left, in this order:
  - EMSA-PKCS1-v1_5 and EMSA-PSS encoding, and the signature API's RSA private keys above them, modelled as `proofs/Rind/X86_64/RsaDispatch.lean` models the public ones, then RSA-2048 signing's ✓ in `verified.toml`.
  - OAEP's encoding and its constant-time decoding.
  - RSA-4096's private operation: a theorem like `private_op_rsa2048_spec`, `private_op_rsa4096_spec`, that discharges the 64-word products [kixel-ai/rind#1143](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/1143) proved (`sqrx8x_spec` with BMI2 and ADX) for 32-word primes, then RSA-4096 signing's ✓ beside RSA-2048's (maintainer, 2026-11-27).
  - `rsa_priv::generate`. Its spec is the key it returns, not how it found it: the components satisfy RFC 8017's relations at the requested size with e = 65537, and d is reduced mod lcm(p - 1, q - 1). Primality is probabilistic and outside a functional spec, as randomness is.
- Other widths stay hypotheses until their products' quests land: aws-lc's `bn_powerx5` with BMI2 and ADX and `bn_power5` without, which `mont_exp` runs at every multiple of eight words but 16 and 32 with BMI2 and ADX, are opaque ([its quest](/quest/m4/verified/rsa-x86-mont.md)); widths that are not a multiple of eight run the proven loop over `mont_mul` and `mont_sqr`'s other products, where `mont_products` holds without `AwsLcMont`, so `mont_exp_by_spec` extends to them once, where the width is not a multiple of four, its table proof takes rows padded to the next multiple of four, gathering the padded row and multiplying by its first `k` words; RSA-4096's public check runs 64-word products, which `AwsLcMont` states; and the dual RSAZ waits on [its quest](/quest/m4/verified/rsa-x86-ifma.md).
- Route: Aeneas to Lean against RFC 8017 (`Rind/Rsa.lean`'s `rsasp1Crt`). Intrinsics are modelled as `Rind/X86_64/Simd.lean` models AES-GCM's. Products elsewhere enter as a hypothesis, as `MontProducts` and `AwsLcMont` in `proofs/Rind/X86_64/RsaMont.lean` do for verification.
- pyca/verified-garbage (VG) measured slower on 2026-11-13 for the private operation with ADX but without IFMA (1.02 to 1.65: every size on the no-AVX-512 tier, and 2048, 2056, 2560, and 4096 bits on the no-IFMA tier) and without ADX (1.4 to 2.2), so rind keeps this code ([the m2 VG epic](/quest/m2/verified-garbage/README.md)). With IFMA, VG won at the sizes the dual RSAZ runs (2048, 3072, and 4096 bits, 0.96 to 0.99) but lost 6144 and 8192 (1.06 and 1.07); on the no-IFMA tier it won 3072, 6144, and 8192 bits but lost the rest. Its switch ([kixel-ai/rind#814](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/814)) runs VG's CRT for 2048- and 4096-bit keys with IFMA, and 3072-bit ones with AVX512_FP16, under the padding glue and fault check this quest proves, in place of `mid::rsa_priv`'s CRT there; VG's lost 3072 bits on Zen 5 (1.21 to 1.48), where the dual RSAZ stays, as it does for the 3969- to 4032-bit keys VG's CRT does not take.

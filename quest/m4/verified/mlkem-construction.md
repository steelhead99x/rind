# [XL] ML-KEM-768's construction is proven

## Goal

ML-KEM-768 key generation and decapsulation earn the ✓ on aarch64 and on x86_64 with AVX2 but without AVX-512, and encapsulation on aarch64 without the SHA-3 instructions, where rind's arithmetic runs; pyca/verified-garbage's code runs the rest, which [its own quest](/quest/m4/verified/mlkem-vg.md) proves. rind's FIPS 203 construction is proven down to s2n-bignum's and mlkem-native's proven polynomial routines and the proven sponge, whose theorems (`shake128x4_squeeze_spec`, `shake256_batch_spec`, `squeezing_squeeze_spec`, and the SHA-3 contexts') `proofs/Rind/X86_64/Sha3.lean` states. rind-rustls's default key exchange, X25519MLKEM768, then earns it wherever X25519 has it.

## Plan

- Runs: `mid/mlkem.rs` and `mid/mlkem/sample_ntt.rs` (about 1,300 lines: K-PKE, the Fujisaki-Okamoto transform with its constant-time compare and select, CBD sampling, SampleNTT's driver, encapsulation-key checks), `low/generic/mlkem.rs`, and the Rust byte decoding on aarch64 (frombytes), over s2n-bignum's NTT, inverse NTT, base multiplication, mulcache, reduce, tomont, tobytes, frombytes, unpack, and rejection sampling, and mlkem-native's compression, AVX2 on x86_64 and NEON on aarch64, all proven in HOL Light.
- Done on aarch64: `admin/prove` translates `mid::mlkem` with the routines' wrappers opaque; `Rind/Aarch64/MlKemRoutines.lean` restates the routines' HOL Light theorems as axioms (ML-KEM-768's only: no `_k2`, `_k4`, `_d5`, or `_d11`), checks rind's zeta tables against s2n-bignum's in the kernel, and states each routine in FIPS 203's terms, tobytes as `ByteEncode_12` and compression as `ByteEncode_d(Compress_d)`; `admin/prove` checks the rejection table's text, too long a literal for Lean. `Rind/Aarch64/MlKemPoly.lean` proves the vector operations (NTT, mulcache, inverse NTT, dot product, reduction, 12-bit coding, sums and differences), and `Rind/Aarch64/MlKemPke.lean` proves `kpke_decrypt` is `K_PKE.Decrypt` for ML-KEM-768 (`kpke_decrypt_fips`), taking the untranslated `Params` impl as a parameter that `Is768` describes. `proofs/Rind/X86_64/MlKemCoding.lean` proves decompression, the message coding, and CBD sampling on both targets, `range_loop` is the rule for a counted loop, and `Rind/MlKemCoding.lean` gives FIPS 203's coding bit by bit.
- Left on aarch64, in `MlKemPke.lean`'s style:
  - `sample_poly_cbd`'s driver over `shake256_batch_spec`, for η = 2 and batches of one to four;
  - `CoeffsMNtt::mul_and_inverse_ntt`, the vector sum, `compress_d10_vector`, and the ciphertext's assembly, then `Decoded::kpke_encrypt` as `K_PKE.Encrypt`, given a `Decoded` whose matrix and `t̂` are the encapsulation key's;
  - SampleNTT's driver over `shake128x4_squeeze_spec` and the rejection axiom, bridged to SampleNTT's loop, whose unbounded iteration needs a bound or a hypothesis;
  - `kpke_keygen`, the encapsulation-key decoding and checks (`EncapKey::parse_mitigated` as `Encaps.KeyCheck`), and `decaps_rind` and `encaps_rind`, over the SHA-3 contexts' and sponges' specs and `ct_equal_spec`, composed to `KeyGen_internal`, `Encaps_internal`, and `Decaps_internal` from `keygen_mitigated`, `decaps_mitigated`, and `encaps_mitigated`.
- Left on x86_64: the same over AVX2, from the routine axioms up (s2n-bignum's x86_64 theorems, mlkem-native's AVX2 compression with its bridge to `Compress`, frombytes to `ByteDecode`, and unpack to `avx2Layout`). x86_64's eight-way matrix sampler, which only ML-KEM-1024 runs, is opaque.
- [kixel-ai/rind#858](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/858) changes `sample_ntt`'s split into eights and assumes `kpke_keygen`, `decaps_rind`, and `encaps_rind` are correct, so keep those names.
- SymCrypt's own proofs ([README-VERIFIEDCRYPTO.md](https://github.com/microsoft/SymCrypt/blob/c2e575ace0ea4b6b7a4184c1f19b81d1d5b2b5be/README-VERIFIEDCRYPTO.md), about 38,000 lines for 2,125 of Rust) cover its Rust, not rind's; reuse its encoding and compression lemmas where their shape fits.

# [L] Ed25519 verification on x86_64 is proven again

## Goal

`ed25519-verify` earns the ✓ again on x86_64 wherever s2n-bignum's point routines run it (with ADX, and Intel CPUs with AVX but not AVX2): rind's Lean proof covers the half-size check `mid::ed25519::VerifyingKey::commits` from the signature API down to s2n-bignum's contracts, and `ed25519_public_key_new` and `ed25519_key_pair_verify` hold on x86_64 as they do on aarch64.

## Plan

- What runs, since [kixel-ai/rind#768](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/768): `mid::half_size::split`, the extended Euclidean algorithm on `8L` and `k`, stopped halfway, giving `u ≡ v k (mod 8L)` with `v` odd and below 2^128; then `low::generic::edwards25519_msm`, Straus's method over width-5 non-adjacent forms of `u` and `v`, tables of `-R`'s and `±A`'s odd multiples, and width-8 forms over static 64-entry tables of `B`'s and `[2^128]B`'s odd multiples, on s2n-bignum's `edwards25519_epadd`, `edwards25519_epdouble`, and `edwards25519_pdouble` (and their `_alt` twins), whose HOL Light theorems hold every output fully reduced; then the identity test `X = 0, Y = Z`. Aeneas already translates all of it; `low::x86_64::adx_mux::edwards25519_msm` and the field product are opaque in `Rind/X86_64/FunsExternal.lean` until this proves them.
- The correctness argument, in `mid/half_size.rs`'s module doc: every point has order dividing `8L`, so `[v](R' - R) = [v S mod L]B - [v]R - [u]A`, and `v` odd and below `L` is prime to `8L`, so the multiple is the identity exactly when `R' = R`. Prove it over `Rind/Ed25519.lean`'s group, which needs the group's exponent, not just `B`'s order: state it as a lemma over HOL Light's `edwards25519_group` and add whatever the spec lacks.
- The split's halving only bounds the work. Correctness needs, through every step, each row's `r ≡ ±t k (mod 8L)` and the two rows' `t` coprime, as `split`'s comments argue from the unimodular matrix; not `t` odd, since the starting row `(8L, 0)` has `t = 0`. Then, for the row `split` returns only, `t` odd (one of two coprime `t` is) and below `L`, and `|u| < 2^255`, the multiplication's precondition: prove those, not that the parts come out near 128 bits.
- The four-point loop needs the s2n-bignum point routines' contracts as axioms, as `Rind/X86_64/S2nBignum.lean` states the others, and the static tables proven to hold `B`'s and `[2^128]B`'s odd multiples, as `Rind/Aarch64/P256Table.lean` proves P-256's.
- Then move `mid_verify_spec` and the three theorems above it back from `Rind/Aarch64/Ed25519Verify.lean` into the shared `Rind/X86_64/Ed25519.lean`, once [the aarch64 measurement](/quest/m2/speed/ed25519-half-scalar-aarch64.md) settles which check aarch64 runs, and give `ed25519-verify` back to x86_64's ✓ rows in `benchmarks/verified.toml`: Intel's AVX without AVX2, and AVX2 with ADX, which [kixel-ai/rind#629](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/629) proved over s2n-bignum's `edwards25519_scalarmuldouble` and [kixel-ai/rind#768](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/768) dropped (maintainer, [2026-11-15](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/768#issuecomment-6889289395): land the speedup and drop the ✓ until this proves the new check ✅, rejected: hold x86_64 on the proven routine until then; remeasure first). The AVX2 sign row's proof text then names verification and the routines it adds again.

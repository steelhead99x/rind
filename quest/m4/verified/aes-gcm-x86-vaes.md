# [XL] AES-GCM on x86_64 with VAES is proven

## Goal

AES-GCM earns the ✓ on x86_64 CPUs with VAES and VPCLMULQDQ (Ice Lake and later, Zen 3 and later), the common tier on today's x86_64 servers and desktops: BoringSSL's VAES kernels, and rind's choice among them, Vale's, and its own, are proven.

## Plan

- Runs (`low/x86_64/aes_gcm_vaes.rs`): BoringSSL's `aes_gcm_{enc,dec}_update_vaes_avx2` from 256 bytes, and `aes_gcm_{enc,dec}_update_vaes_avx512` from 2 KB where the CPU has AVX-512, with their key-power setup `gcm_init_vpclmulqdq_avx2` and `_avx512` in BoringSSL's table layout; below those lengths, rind's by-8 and AVX-512 by-16 intrinsics (proven, given this tier's key powers), and Vale's proven routines for opens under 256 bytes on cores without AVX-512.
- Already proven: Vale's routines, the AEAD API's glue on Vale's tier, and nothing of BoringSSL's. No published proof of a VAES or AVX-512 AES-GCM routine exists, and Vale has none.
- Route: Lean proofs of BoringSSL's four kernels and two key setups on VG's x86_64 model, with their leakage contracts, as [kixel-ai/rind#912](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/912) proved aarch64 SHA-256. VG's model already has EVEX `vaesenc`, `vaesenclast`, and `vpclmulqdq`; model what the kernels use beyond it, such as the VEX.256 forms, in `proofs/verified-garbage/RindIsa/`. The first x86_64 proof also extends `rind_isa` and `vg --rind` in `admin/prove`, aarch64-only today, to x86_64.
- The spec: `common/gcm.ml` and `common/fips197.ml` at rind's s2n-bignum pin, `3f01260f`, which has [awslabs/s2n-bignum#490](https://github.com/awslabs/s2n-bignum/pull/490)'s rewrite of both and their known-answer tests (`common/gcm_kats.ml`, `common/fips197_kats.ml`).
- Then Aeneas to Lean for rind's glue around the kernels and the length-based choice, as `Rind/X86_64/AesGcm.lean` does for Vale's tier.
- The two GCM specs (maintainer, 2026-11-09): ✅ prove them equal in Lean (rejected: amend the composition rule to allow a spec-equivalence axiom; leave it for the quest to raise). This tier mixes kernels proven against s2n-bignum's `gcm_ae` and `gcm_ad` (`common/gcm.ml:149`, `:169` at rind's pin) with Vale's routines and the theorems of `Rind/X86_64/AesGcm.lean`, stated over Vale's `gcm_encrypt_LE` and `gcm_decrypt_LE`. So no axiom relates the two:
  - Transliterate both under the composition rule, as a function a proof must compute. Vale's side is done: `gcmEncrypt`, `gcmDecrypt`, and `computeIvBE` in `proofs/Rind/Vale.lean` are their `Vale.AES.GCM_s` definitions, not opaque constants (checked 2026-11-12), so reuse them. s2n-bignum's are its HOL Light definitions over `common/fips197.ml`'s AES.
  - `sbox` and `mixColumns` in `proofs/Rind/Vale.lean` are still `opaque`, implemented by `fips197Sbox` and `fips197MixColumns`. A proof must be able to unfold them to FIPS 197's definitions, or the two specs' block ciphers cannot be shown to be one function.
  - One theorem each for encryption and decryption: for every key length both specs take (Vale's `Algorithm` has AES-192 already), 96-bit IV, AAD, and message within both specs' bounds, the two compute the same ciphertext and tag, and accept the same tags. Most of it is byte and bit order: Vale's little-endian `quad32` and reversed GF(2^128) elements against SP 800-38D's bit strings.
  - It goes in a file no target owns, beside `Rind/Vale.lean` and `Rind/Gf128.lean`, so every later quest that meets both specs imports it: [AES-GCM on aarch64](/quest/m4/verified/aes-gcm-aarch64.md) and [its AES-192 kernels](/quest/m4/verified/aes-gcm-aarch64-192.md), [aarch64 without the crypto extension](/quest/m5/verified/aarch64-no-crypto.md), and [x86_64 without AES-NI](/quest/m5/verified/x86-no-aesni.md). If one of those lands first, it writes the theorem there and this quest reuses it.
  - Size stays XL, the largest there is; the theorem adds to it.
- The AVX-512 tier below 2 KB is proven over two predicates this quest discharges for `gcm_init_vpclmulqdq_avx512`: `Avx512Powers H t`, that `VaesKernel::powers` gives the hash key's 16 descending powers, and `TableFor H t.clmul`, that the PCLMULQDQ table `from_powers` builds holds the first eight. Given them, `tagged_from_avx512_spec` (`proofs/Rind/X86_64/AesGcmAvx512.lean`) is `tagged_from_spec`'s ciphertext and tag under 2 KB, by-16 from 256 bytes and by-8 below, so the tier's seals and opens compose it with the kernel's proof from 2 KB, as `Rind/X86_64/AesGcmClmul.lean` composes `tagged_from_spec`.
- Do the AVX2 kernel first: it is the whole path on Zen 3 and on cores without AVX-512.

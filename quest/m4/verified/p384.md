# [L] P-384 ECDH and ECDSA are proven on x86_64 and aarch64

## Goal

P-384 key agreement and ECDSA earn the ✓ on x86_64 and aarch64, from the agreement and signature APIs to s2n-bignum's routines, as P-256's do on aarch64.

## Plan

- Runs: s2n-bignum's `p384_montjscalarmul` for d·Q and its field, point, and scalar routines, all proven; rind's Rust comb for k·G (20 tables of 16 affine points over Booth-5 windows, `mid/p384/precomp.rs`), the affine table select (AVX2 or SSE2 intrinsics on x86_64, NEON on aarch64), verification's single-chain wNAF double multiplication, to-affine, decoding, and encoding.
- Route: Aeneas to Lean, reusing P-256's proof structure (`Rind/P256Group.lean`, `P256Multiples.lean`, `P256Ecdsa.lean`) over a P-384 curve: Pratt certificates for p and n, G's order, and a table proof like `Rind/Aarch64/P256Table.lean` for the comb.
- The select intrinsics: model them as `Rind/X86_64/Simd.lean` does, or replace them with s2n-bignum's proven `bignum_copy_row_from_table` if that measures as fast.
- Verification settles equal and opposite points by cases before s2n-bignum's additions, whose proofs exclude them; prove those cases as P-256's proof does.
- A parsed public key's order reuses [the curve proofs' group assumptions](/quest/m4/verified/curve-groups.md): `nist_order` in `proofs/Rind/P256Order.lean`, with P-384's own no-root certificate. P-256's certificate has landed.

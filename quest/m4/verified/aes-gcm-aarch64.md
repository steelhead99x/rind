# [L] AES-GCM on aarch64 is proven

## Goal

AES-GCM earns the ✓ on aarch64 with the crypto extension (Graviton, Apple, Ampere, and the other Neoverse cores): rind's key setup, its short-message pass, and its glue around s2n-bignum's proven kernels are proven from the AEAD API down.

## Plan

- Runs (`low/aarch64/aes_gcm.rs`, `aes.rs`, `ghash.rs`): rind's NEON key expansion, PMULL GHASH key table, J0 and counter, AAD GHASH, partial blocks, length block, and tag; s2n-bignum's kernels for whole blocks; and up to 15 blocks of AAD and message sealed or 31 opened, each rounded up separately, rind's own one-pass Rust, which encrypts every counter block and the tag's and hashes with one reduction per eight blocks.
- Already proven, below the AEAD API: the PMULL GHASH (`Rind/Aarch64/GhashPmull.lean`); the one-pass `short` (`Rind/Aarch64/AesGcmShort.lean`); `counter_block` and `initial_counter` as `compute_iv_BE`, and `tagged_from` and `tagged` at `short`'s lengths with every key size (`Rind/Aarch64/AesGcm.lean`), none on an axiom; and, with AES-128 and AES-256 keys, `cipher` as GCTR and GHASH from any counter block, `tagged_from` at every length as GCTR and the tag on `gcm_hash`, and `tagged` with a 96-bit nonce as `gcm_encrypt_LE` and `gcm_decrypt_LE` (`Rind/Aarch64/AesGcmLong.lean`), on the eight s2n-bignum kernels' theorems at rind's pin (`3f01260f`), restated as axioms in `Rind/Aarch64/S2nBignum.lean` over `Rind/S2nGcm.lean`'s transliteration of their spec, which it proves is Vale's. On cores with SHA3, where the table has `eight_way`, these theorems take the hypothesis `KernelBuffersLow` (`haddr`): the `_alt` kernels' requirement that the blocks' address plus their length is below 2^63, which a pointer tag that sets bit 63 breaks, and which nothing discharges.
- Already translated: `aes_gcm.rs` and `ghash.rs` through Aeneas, from `AesGcm::new`, `encrypt`, `decrypt`, `encrypt_any`, and `decrypt_any` down, with `Rind/Aarch64/AesGcmModel.lean` modelling by hand, quoting the Rust, the NEON transmutes, `Ghash::add`'s chunking, the one-pass `short` with its `Aggregate`, and `stitched`, the kernels' dispatch.
- Left to prove: AES-192 past the short path on cores without SHA3, where `cipher` runs `composed`, AES-CTR and GHASH in turn; prove it GCTR and GHASH as `cipher_spec` states them, so `tagged_from_spec`, `tagged_seal_spec`, and `tagged_open_spec` in `Rind/Aarch64/AesGcmLong.lean` drop their `alg ≠ .AES_192` for tables without `eight_way`. Then the AEAD API above `tagged`: `AesGcm::new`, `encrypt`, `decrypt` (whose `ct_equal` `Rind/Aarch64/CtEqual.lean` covers), `encrypt_any`, and `decrypt_any`, as `AesGcmAead`, `AesGcmAny`, and `GcmKey` do on x86_64.
- Left to prove: `KernelBuffersLow` on cores with SHA3: guard `stitched`'s `_alt` dispatch on it and prove the guard, or state the pointer bound of every supported target, whichever the maintainer picks. Android's Bionic heap tags (`0xB4` in the top byte) already put aarch64 Android's buffers outside the bound, so a proof limited to targets that meet it excludes Android.
- Route: Aeneas to Lean, as on x86_64, with the kernels' theorems as axioms under the composition rule. Reuse `Rind/Aarch64/AesModesLow.lean`'s CTR proofs and `GhashPmull.lean`'s `ghash_add_spec` for `composed`.
- AES-192 with the SHA3 instructions runs aws-lc's `aesv8_gcm_8x_enc_192` and `aesv8_gcm_8x_dec_192` since [kixel-ai/rind#460](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/460), which nothing proves: [their own quest](/quest/m4/verified/aes-gcm-aarch64-192.md) does, so AES-192-GCM earns the ✓ on those cores only once it lands, and this quest's ✓ for AES-192 names the cores without SHA3.

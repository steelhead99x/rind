# [L] AES-GCM on aarch64 is proven

## Goal

AES-GCM earns the ✓ on aarch64 with the crypto extension (Graviton, Apple, Ampere, and the other Neoverse cores): rind's key setup, its short-message pass, and its glue around s2n-bignum's proven kernels are proven from the AEAD API down.

## Plan

- Runs (`low/aarch64/aes_gcm.rs`, `aes.rs`, `ghash.rs`): rind's NEON key expansion, PMULL GHASH key table, J0 and counter, AAD GHASH, partial blocks, length block, and tag; s2n-bignum's kernels for whole blocks; and up to 15 blocks of AAD and message sealed or 31 opened, each rounded up separately, rind's own one-pass Rust, which encrypts every counter block and the tag's and hashes with one reduction per eight blocks.
- Already proven: the kernels, against SP 800-38D, in place or out, by s2n-bignum's theorems at rind's pin (`3f01260f`), which `admin/prove bytes` checks are of rind's code. Of rind's Rust, the PMULL GHASH: `GhashTable::new`'s key powers and `Ghash::add`, as Vale's `ghash_LE` (`Rind/Aarch64/GhashPmull.lean`). The one-pass `short`, as Vale's `gctr_encrypt_LE` and `gcm_hash` (`short_spec` in `Rind/Aarch64/AesGcmShort.lean`), and above it `counter_block` and `initial_counter` as `compute_iv_BE`, `tagged_from` for the lengths `short` takes as GCTR and the tag on `gcm_hash` from the counter block it is given, and `tagged` with a 96-bit nonce at those lengths as `gcm_encrypt_LE` and `gcm_decrypt_LE` (`Rind/Aarch64/AesGcm.lean`); none rests on an axiom.
- Already translated: `aes_gcm.rs` and `ghash.rs` through Aeneas, from `AesGcm::new`, `encrypt`, `decrypt`, `encrypt_any`, and `decrypt_any` down, with `Rind/Aarch64/AesGcmModel.lean` modelling by hand, quoting the Rust, the NEON transmutes, `Ghash::add`'s chunking, and the one-pass `short` with its `Aggregate`, and `cipher` (the kernels' dispatch) left opaque.
- Left to prove: `cipher`, with a hand model quoting its dispatch and the kernels' theorems as axioms, which needs [aes-gcm-x86-vaes](/quest/m4/verified/aes-gcm-x86-vaes.md)'s theorem that s2n-bignum's GCM spec (`aes_ctr_block`, `nist_ghash`, `htable_mem_4` or, for the `_alt` kernels the SHA3 cores run, `htable_mem_8` of `ghash_twist`) is Vale's. That theorem must hold at the GCTR and GHASH level from any counter block, since `tagged_from` hands `cipher` the counter block of an IV of any length, not only a 96-bit nonce's; aes-gcm-x86-vaes states it end to end for 96-bit IVs, so this quest proves that counter-level form if aes-gcm-x86-vaes doesn't. Then `tagged_from`'s long branch, whose length block, tag mask, and final XOR mirror `Rind/X86_64/AesGcmClmul.lean`'s `tagged_from_spec`, joined with `tagged_from_short_spec` into one theorem at every length; and the AEAD API above, `AesGcm::new`, `encrypt`, `decrypt` (whose `ct_equal` `Rind/Aarch64/CtEqual.lean` covers), `encrypt_any`, and `decrypt_any`, as `AesGcmAead`, `AesGcmAny`, and `GcmKey` do on x86_64.
- Route: Aeneas to Lean, as `Rind/X86_64/AesGcm.lean` proves Vale's tier, with the kernels' theorems as axioms under the composition rule, and NEON intrinsics (`aese`, `aesmc`, `pmull`, `pmull2`, `eor3`, `ext`) modelled from the Arm manual as `Simd.lean` models Intel's. Reuse `Gf128.lean`'s Karatsuba lemmas, and `Rind/Aarch64/Neon.lean`'s AES models and `Rind/Aarch64/AesKey.lean`'s key expansion proofs.
- AES-192, which s2n-bignum's kernels lack, takes one of two paths past the short pass's lengths (`low/aarch64/aes_gcm.rs:259-264`, `:305-312`). Without the SHA3 instructions it runs AES-CTR and GHASH in turn; prove that composition here too. With them it runs aws-lc's `aesv8_gcm_8x_enc_192` and `aesv8_gcm_8x_dec_192` since [kixel-ai/rind#460](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/460), which nothing proves: [their own quest](/quest/m4/verified/aes-gcm-aarch64-192.md) does, so AES-192-GCM earns the ✓ on those cores only once it lands, and this quest's ✓ for AES-192 names the cores without SHA3.

# [L] P-521 ECDH and ECDSA are proven on x86_64 and aarch64

## Goal

P-521 key agreement and ECDSA earn the ✓ on x86_64 and aarch64, from the agreement and signature APIs to s2n-bignum's routines.

## Plan

- Runs: s2n-bignum's `p521_jscalarmul` and its field, point, and scalar routines, all proven; rind's Rust comb for k·G (27 tables of 16 affine points, `mid/p521/precomp.rs`), which selects around the cases where `p521_jadd`'s proof excludes equal, opposite, or infinite points, the affine select intrinsics, verification's double multiplication, to-affine, decoding, and encoding.
- Route: as P-384's (`proofs/Rind/X86_64/P384Keygen.lean`, `P384Ecdsa.lean`, `P384Signature.lean`), whose curve-generic lemmas this reuses; P-521's field is direct rather than Montgomery's.
- pyca/verified-garbage (VG) measured slower on 2026-11-13 (P-521 1.03 to 1.69x rind's time on every x86_64 and aarch64 tier, the ones this quest covers), so rind keeps this code ([the m2 VG epic](/quest/m2/verified-garbage/README.md)).
- Tranches: decided by the owner on 2026-11-25, each tranche lands on its own as it is ready, and this quest stays open for the rest (rejected: hold every tranche until the whole quest is done). Each tranche's PR rewrites the lists below.
- Done, first tranche ([steelhead99x/rind#250](https://github.com/steelhead99x/rind/pull/250)): P-521's parameters in `proofs/Rind/Nist.lean` (`p521`, `n521`, `b521`, `p521G`, the group operations and the ECDSA abbreviations, from HOL Light's `EC/nistp521.ml`), and `proofs/Rind/P521Group.lean`: `p521` and `n521` are prime by Pratt certificates, G is on the curve, n·G is infinity, and every multiple of G by [1, n) is a finite point of order n. That is the spec side only: no rind code is proven yet, and no check mark changes.
- Left, in order:

  - rind's field and point routines against s2n-bignum's proofs (`p521_jadd`, `p521_jdouble`, `p521_jmixadd`, `bignum_*_p521`) on x86_64 and aarch64, with `p521Represents` as a direct, not Montgomery, representation, and the Rust glue.
  - The comb for k·G (`mid/p521/precomp.rs`, 27 tables) and its select, `p521_jscalarmul`, verification's double multiplication, to-affine, decoding, and encoding.
  - The ECDH and ECDSA entry points' theorems on both targets, the `benchmarks/verified.toml` rows and the check marks; the PR that lands them deletes this quest.

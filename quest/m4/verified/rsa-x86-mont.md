# [L] x86_64's other RSA Montgomery products are proven

## Goal

Every RSA Montgomery product x86_64 runs outside s2n-bignum's Karatsuba routines is proven in Lean on VG's x86_64 model, so RSA-3072, RSA-4096, and every size on CPUs without ADX earn the ✓ once the glue is proven.

## Plan

- Done: `bn_mul_mont_nohw`, the fallback for every width from two words, is proven whole in `proofs/hol-light/x86_64/bn_mul_mont_nohw.ml` (`BN_MUL_MONT_NOHW_SUBROUTINE_CORRECT`), with its stack frame, probes, and callee-saved registers, against s2n-bignum's `bignum_montmul` statement.
- Runs left (`low/x86_64/mont.rs`, `rsaz.rs`): aws-lc's `bn_mul4x_mont`, `bn_mulx4x_mont`, `bn_sqr8x_mont` over `bn_sqr8x_internal` and `bn_sqrx8x_internal`, `bn_power5` and `bn_powerx5` (with rind's two zeroed scratch words), and on CPUs with AVX2 but without ADX, RSAZ AVX2's `rsaz_1024_{mul,sqr,gather5,scatter5,norm2red,red2norm}_avx2` for RSA-2048's primes. They measured 1.1 to 3 times as fast as s2n-bignum's generic routines.
- Route: Lean on VG's x86_64 model, which has `mulx`, `adcx`, and `adox`, with leakage contracts: the products and squarings, `bn_power5` and `bn_powerx5` as the chain of them they run, against the same statement as `bn_mul_mont_nohw`'s, and RSAZ's gather, scatter, and limb conversions against statements of their own, as [kixel-ai/rind#912](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/912) proved aarch64 SHA-256; model what the routines use beyond VG's in `proofs/verified-garbage/RindIsa/`. The 4x routines share `bn_mul_mont_nohw`'s frame and probe prologue, whose HOL Light lemmas (`FRAME_ALIGN`, `PROBE_SPAN`, the probe loop) show what their Lean ones state; [the m5 port](/quest/m5/verified/hol-light-to-lean.md) moves `bn_mul_mont_nohw` itself. RSAZ AVX2 needs the 29-bit-limb representation's lemmas. The first x86_64 proof also extends `rind_isa` and `vg --rind` in `admin/prove`, aarch64-only today, to x86_64.
- Import the theorems as axioms, retiring for these paths the `AwsLcMont` hypothesis `proofs/Rind/X86_64/RsaMont.lean`'s `mont_products` takes for RSA verification. `bn_mul_mont_nohw`'s can go in now: an axiom on the opaque `low.x86_64.mont.nohw` drops `AwsLcMont`'s first conjunct, once `MontArgs` bounds the width below `2^32`, which the routine's `mov r9d, r9d` needs and RSA's key-size limit gives.
- Upstream: offering this work to awslabs/s2n-bignum is optional, and there is no standing approval: stop and ask the maintainer before posting there or anywhere outside [the orgs AGENTS.md lists](/AGENTS.md#development) (decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-07 on [kixel-ai/rind#448](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/448)).
- pyca/verified-garbage (VG) measured slower on 2026-11-13 wherever these routines run, at one size or more: signing with ADX but without IFMA 1.02 to 1.65 (the no-AVX-512 and no-IFMA tiers; VG won 3072, 6144, and 8192 bits on no-IFMA, 0.84 to 0.91, but lost 2048, 2056, 2560, and 4096), RSA-6144 and RSA-8192 signing with IFMA 1.06 to 1.07, and without ADX 1.4 to 2.2, so rind keeps this code ([the m2 VG epic](/quest/m2/verified-garbage/README.md)).

# [L] x86_64's other RSA Montgomery products are proven

## Goal

Every RSA Montgomery product x86_64 runs outside s2n-bignum's Karatsuba routines is proven in Lean on VG's x86_64 model, so RSA-3072, RSA-4096, and every size on CPUs without ADX earn the ✓ once the glue is proven.

## Plan

- Done: `bn_mul_mont_nohw`, the fallback for every width from two words, is proven whole in `proofs/hol-light/x86_64/bn_mul_mont_nohw.ml` (`BN_MUL_MONT_NOHW_SUBROUTINE_CORRECT`), with its stack frame, probes, and callee-saved registers, against s2n-bignum's `bignum_montmul` statement, and `proofs/Rind/X86_64/RsaMont.lean` imports it as the axiom `nohw_spec`, so `mont_products` holds without `AwsLcMont` at every width under eight words or not a multiple of four, and `AwsLcMont` states only the 4x and 8x routines.
- Done: `bn_mul4x_mont`, the 4x product without BMI2 and ADX, is proven in Lean on VG's x86_64 model in `proofs/Rind/X86_64/Mul4xProof.lean` (`bn_mul4x_mont_correct`), with its frame of up to `8k + 1104` bytes, page walk, callee-saved registers, and `ConstantTime`, and `RsaMont.lean` imports it as the axiom `mul4x_spec`, so `AwsLcMont` states only `bn_mulx4x_mont` and `bn_sqr8x_mont`. `proofs/verified-garbage/RindIsa/X86_64.lean` extends VG's model with the stack moves, pushes, `lea`, `neg`, `dec`, aligned 128-bit moves, `ja`, and padding the routine uses, and `admin/prove`'s `rind_isa` and `vg --rind` check x86_64's programs' bytes against the Linux build and cosimulate the extension (`Cosim/RindX86_64.lean`).
- Done: `bn_mulx4x_mont`, the 4x product with BMI2 and ADX, is proven in Lean in `proofs/Rind/X86_64/Mulx4xProof.lean` (`bn_mulx4x_mont_correct`) for widths below `2^29`, since it scales the width to bytes in 32 bits, with its frame of up to `8k + 248` bytes, page walk, callee-saved registers, and `ConstantTime`, and `RsaMont.lean` imports it as the axiom `mulx4x_spec`, so `AwsLcMont` states only `bn_sqr8x_mont`, which `mont_products` takes only at the multiples of eight it squares at, and `MontArgs` bounds every width below `2^29`.
- Done: with BMI2 and ADX, `sqr8x` runs rind's `bn_sqrx8x_mont`, a structured copy of aws-lc's `bn_sqr8x_mont` that always calls `bn_sqrx8x_internal` (`rind/src/low/x86_64/sqrx8x_mont.s`), proven with that routine in Lean in `proofs/Rind/X86_64/Sqrx8xInternal.lean` (`bn_sqrx8x_mont_correct`) for widths below `2^29`, a frame of up to `48k + 184` bytes, and a stack below `2^63`, with `ConstantTime`. rind's x86_64 model gained VG's `call` and `ret`, laid out as `call rel32` with an `R_X86_64_PLT32` relocation that `rind-asm` and `admin/prove lean` check names the proven callee. `RsaMont.lean` imports it as the axiom `sqrx8x_spec`, so `AwsLcMont` states only `bn_sqr8x_mont` without BMI2 and ADX, over `bn_sqr8x_internal`, and `mont_products` holds at every width with them.
- Runs left (`low/x86_64/mont.rs`, `rsaz.rs`): aws-lc's `bn_sqr8x_mont` over `bn_sqr8x_internal`, the squaring without BMI2 and ADX, `bn_power5` and `bn_powerx5` (with rind's two zeroed scratch words), and on CPUs with AVX2 but without ADX, RSAZ AVX2's `rsaz_1024_{mul,sqr,gather5,scatter5,norm2red,red2norm}_avx2` for RSA-2048's primes. They measured 1.1 to 3 times as fast as s2n-bignum's generic routines.
- Route: Lean on VG's x86_64 model, which has `mulx`, `adcx`, and `adox`, with leakage contracts: the products and squarings, `bn_power5` and `bn_powerx5` as the chain of them they run, against the same statement as `bn_mul_mont_nohw`'s, and RSAZ's gather, scatter, and limb conversions against statements of their own, as [kixel-ai/rind#912](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/912) proved aarch64 SHA-256; model what the routines use beyond VG's in `proofs/verified-garbage/RindIsa/`. The 4x routines share `bn_mul_mont_nohw`'s frame and probe prologue, whose HOL Light lemmas (`FRAME_ALIGN`, `PROBE_SPAN`, the probe loop) show what their Lean ones state; [the m5 port](/quest/m5/verified/hol-light-to-lean.md) moves `bn_mul_mont_nohw` itself. RSAZ AVX2 needs the 29-bit-limb representation's lemmas. `bn_mul4x_mont`'s, `bn_mulx4x_mont`'s, and `bn_sqrx8x_mont`'s proofs show the shape: `bn_sqr8x_internal` is `bn_sqrx8x_internal`'s squaring and reduction by `mul` and `adc`, so the `Sqrx8x*` files' phase structure, `Sqrx8xSpec`'s contract, and `Sqrx8xMontProof`'s `Internal` hypothesis carry over, with a structured copy of `bn_sqr8x_mont` for CPUs without them as `sqrx8x_mont.s` is for CPUs with them. `bn_powerx5` calls `bn_sqrx8x_internal` five times, so its proof can take `bn_sqrx8x_internal_correct` wherever its frame meets `InPre`.
- Import the rest as axioms on the opaque `sqr8x`, as `mul4x_spec`, `mulx4x_spec`, and `sqrx8x_spec` restate theirs, retiring the `AwsLcMont` hypothesis `proofs/Rind/X86_64/RsaMont.lean`'s `mont_products` takes for RSA verification, and [RSA signing](/quest/m4/verified/rsa-sign.md) may take for its products.

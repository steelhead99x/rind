# [M] SLH-DSA's SHAKE sets on x86_64 match the SPHINCS+ AVX2 C

## Goal

SLH-DSA's SHAKE sets sign and verify on x86_64 with AVX2 at least as fast as the SPHINCS+ team's AVX2 build, which they now trail by up to 1.2x.

## Plan

- Cause, measured on [kixel-ai/rind#641](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/641): about 85% of SHAKE time is the four-way Keccak-f. s2n-bignum's verified `sha3_keccak4_f1600_alt` transposes its four states in and out on every call (257 ns); XKCP's `KeccakP1600times4`, which SPHINCS+ runs on states it keeps transposed, takes 240 ns.
- Route: port XKCP's AVX2 times4 permutation (CC0) and keep `shake256_x4`'s states in its interleaved layout across calls, so only absorb and squeeze transpose. `AGENTS.md` says to run the fastest source and plan its proof, so this quest also writes the proof plan into `ALGORITHMS.md`, or a quest for it under [verified](/quest/m4/verified/README.md).
- Decision (maintainer, 2026-11-13): ✅ port XKCP's four-way Keccak and plan its proof (rejected: keep s2n-bignum's verified permutation and accept the gap; drop this quest).
- Done on this branch ([kixel-ai/rind#872](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/872)), 2026-11-18: the port, `low::x86_64::sha3_keccak4x_avx2`, on states `shake256_x4` keeps interleaved, compiled once for AVX2 and once for AVX-512 VL. On an AVX-512 Xeon (2 cores, shared) it took 0.6x the time of s2n-bignum's permutation for SHAKE signing and 0.7-0.8x for verifying with AVX-512 VL, and the same with AVX2 alone. The proof plan is in `ALGORITHMS.md`'s SLH-DSA section.
- What remains, measured on an AVX2-only Intel Core Ultra 9 275HX (Arrow Lake-HX) against SPHINCS+'s `shake-avx2` built with `-march=haswell` (minimum of six interleaved rounds): 128s sign 212.0 vs 180.1 ms (1.18x), verify 241 vs 234 µs (1.03x); 128f sign 9.49 vs 8.43 ms (1.13x), verify 634 vs 597 µs (1.06x); 256f sign 31.5 vs 27.6 ms (1.14x), verify 949 vs 872 µs (1.09x). The earlier 1.4x compared an AVX-512 SPHINCS+ build on a Xeon, which rind's AVX2 port does not match. One-block inputs (every F, H, and PRF) now move into and out of the four-way vectors through a 4x4 transpose in registers (`sha3_keccak4x_hash_blocks`), which took signing 0-4% and verification 5-9% faster. The permutation itself is 5-8% behind the C and about 54 ns per call around it is unexplained: profile it with `perf` or hardware counters, which that host lacked. The one-state SHAKE verification uses is at parity (211 vs 211 ns). Tried without gain: unrolling the permutation loop, fixed-size copies of the part lengths, and sharing the rounds as a function (the permutation got 10% slower).
- Open on the landed port (a second model's review of `d5c65137` found them; none is fixed on this branch yet): no `ct_sde_*` test covers the AVX-512 VL copy (`permute_vl`, `hash_blocks_vl`), which runs on SK.seed-derived input and which valgrind cannot decode, so `admin/ct-sde` needs one wired into the `x86_64-sde` job and listed in TESTING.md; `LICENSE` and its five synced copies do not list XKCP (CC0-1.0), which `ALGORITHMS.md` already does; `.cargo/mutants.toml` has no exclusion for the AVX-512 VL arms that hosted runners cannot reach, or for the one-block guards whose two paths give identical digests.
- Open (maintainer): the acceptance below is unmet by 1.03-1.18x on AVX2. Either profile the 54 ns with hardware counters (`perf_event_paranoid` 1 or lower), or accept the gap and amend this quest's Done-when.
- Scope (recommended): SLH-DSA only. ML-KEM and ML-DSA keep s2n-bignum's proven permutation unless the port measures faster for them too, since switching them would cost their ✓.
- Done when `rind-bench/benches/slhdsa.rs` shows SHAKE signing and verification at or below SPHINCS+'s AVX2 times on the same host, and `ALGORITHMS.md`'s SLH-DSA note is updated.

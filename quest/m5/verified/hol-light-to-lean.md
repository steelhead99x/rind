# [XL] rind's HOL Light proofs are ported to Lean

## Goal

Every proof in `proofs/hol-light/` is a Lean theorem on pyca/verified-garbage's instruction models, functional correctness and a leakage contract alike, about the bytes rind's build emits. Each axiom in `proofs/Rind/<Arch>/` that restated one restates the Lean theorem instead, still one per wrapper under the composition rule, as [kixel-ai/rind#912](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/912) did for `compress_hw`. `admin/prove hol-light`, its CI jobs, and the DMTCP session tooling go. Upstream s2n-bignum theorems rind imports stay axioms, checked by `admin/prove bytes`.

## Plan

Decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-18 (/quest-plan): ✅ port the existing HOL Light proofs to Lean in m5, once the trial shows the cost (rejected: keep them in HOL Light forever; port them before measuring). Why: the goal is one Lean trust base for rind's own proofs, and porting removes the slowest jobs in `prove`, about 95 minutes of HOL Light per pull request run on 2026-11-17.

- Size, from the trial ([kixel-ai/rind#912](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/912)): its 621-line HOL Light proof of aarch64 SHA-256 took about 940 lines of Lean, a 230-line extension of VG's aarch64 model, and about an hour of one agent session, a third of the HOL Light proof's agent time, and checks in 6 s against HOL Light's 12 s. About 20,800 lines of HOL Light remain, plus what lands in flight, so this stays XL: split it into an epic when it starts, one child per family, each about L: aarch64 (`bn_mul_mont`, 7,133 lines of the total, `sha512_block_data_order_hw`, `bignum_eq`, the compares); x86_64 SHA-2 (the SHA extensions' three files, SHA-512 on AVX2, and SHA-256 on AVX2 once [kixel-ai/rind#636](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/636) lands), whose instructions VG's x86_64 model has; x86_64's bignum and P-256 (`bn_mul_mont_nohw`, the two `ecp_nistz256_point_add_affine` forms); x86_64 AES-CBC; and armv7, with VG's ARMv7 model vendored. The first x86_64 child extends `admin/prove`'s `rind_isa` and `vg --rind` past aarch64.
- The trial's lemmas in `proofs/Rind/Aarch64/Sha256HwAsm.lean` that are not about SHA-256 (`execBlock_append`, `exec_det`, the memory lemmas, `Frame`) move to a shared file once a second proof needs them.
- What the fork of s2n-bignum still carries for rind's own proofs, such as the x86 model additions, the AArch32 model, and `common/chacha20poly1305.ml` with its `hol_light_specs` load, leaves with the last proof that needs it. Its upstream code and theorems rind imports stay.
- armv7's proofs run on the fork's AArch32 model. VG has an ARMv7 model too, but rind vendors only its x86_64 and aarch64 models: vendor ARMv7's through `admin/import-vg-models` and cosimulate it against qemu-arm, as `admin/prove cosim` does today.
- Nothing needs `admin/prove hol-light` once this port is done: armv7's proofs, which have no Lean model in rind yet, are ported here too.
- Each ported proof keeps every check its HOL Light route had, as [kixel-ai/rind#912](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/912) does: the build's instruction bytes, relocations, and statics (such as x86_64 SHA-256's `K` table) are checked against the theorem, which keeps the HOL Light theorem's frame (return address, the registers and memory it may change), and `admin/prove vg` cosimulates every instruction the routine runs before its old model check retires.
- Keep CI's checks as the jobs go: move `admin/prove bytes`, today a step of `hol-light-aarch64`, to a job that stays, and retire `admin/prove cosim` and its job only once no proof rests on the AArch32 model or the fork's x86 additions it also checks, and VG's cosimulation covers armv7.
- Retire each routine's dedicated constant-time harness as its leakage contract lands, as [kixel-ai/rind#912](https://github.com/kixel-ai/rind/pull/912) decided, once a check that stays traces its Rust wrapper on that path, as [the leakage-contracts quest](/quest/m2/verified-garbage/leakage-contracts.md) requires for VG's routines.

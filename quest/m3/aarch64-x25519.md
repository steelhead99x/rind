# [S] aarch64 X25519 is as fast as its sources allow

## Goal

rind's X25519 on aarch64 runs at least as fast as aws-lc-rs on the same pinned core, and its ratio to Ed25519 verify matches what its source predicts, or the phone measurement that suggested otherwise is shown to be noise.

## Plan

Decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-12, merging [moq-dev/rind#666](https://github.com/moq-dev/rind/pull/666): ✅ add this quest in m3 (rejected: merge with no quest).

On a Pixel 10 Pro (Tensor G5) through rind-dart, rind's X25519 measured a 232 µs median (102 to 321 across 5 runs) against Ed25519 verify's 165 µs; on the i7-8700 X25519 is about half of Ed25519 verify (21 vs 40 µs). The phone could not pin the benchmark thread, so the gap may be core migration.

1. Measure on a pinned core of an aarch64 host (`ubuntu-24.04-arm` in CI, or the Mac mini): rind's X25519 against its Ed25519 verify and against aws-lc-rs, with the `rind-bench` harness. Recommendation: start with `ubuntu-24.04-arm` (Neoverse), since it is available now; repeat on the Mac mini when it arrives.
2. If the gap is noise, delete this quest and note the pinned numbers in the PR.
3. If it is real, fix the dispatch or source of `rind/src/low/aarch64/curve25519_x25519.rs` per AGENTS.md's fastest-source rule. It transliterates s2n-bignum's `curve25519_x25519_alt.S`, the variant tuned for cores with fast multiply-high; s2n-bignum's non-`alt` `curve25519_x25519.S` and aws-lc's own aarch64 X25519 are the candidates to measure against it on each core. Recommendation: take whichever is fastest on every measured core, keeping one s2n-bignum variant if it is within noise, since its proof carries over.

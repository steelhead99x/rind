MoQ on SX1276

Live encrypted video over an old SX1276 radio on a Pi 2

A Raspberry Pi 2 and SX1276 radios carry live H.264 video, encrypted with rind. The radio software is rewritten in Rust.

3.8 → ~280kbit/s, stock Python → Rust
0.02%loss, 1,200 B datagrams overnight
+20 Bper frame for encryption
915.0 MHz, +5 dBm, radios about 1 m apart
Project measurements. moq-lora branch video-demo, commit 97f34b7.
MoQ on SX1276

Hardware

Pi side

Raspberry Pi 2 + Dragino LoRa/GPS HAT v1.4

  • armv7, 4 cores at 900 MHz, 1 GB
  • SX1276 radio (RFM95W)
  • Runs ffmpeg and the Rust sender
⇄915.0 MHz+5 dBm
about 1 m
Mac side

CatWAN RP2040 USB stick

  • SX1276-class radio (RFM95W)
  • Rust firmware v5
  • The Mac runs the Rust receiver and ffplay

Either side can transmit.

What these tests do not cover

Settings used

  • 915.0 MHz, +5 dBm
  • Duty-cycle limits not applied in testing

Not measured

  • FSK 6 dB bandwidth against FCC 15.247, which needs at least 500 kHz without hopping

Not proven

  • Range: bench distance only, about 1 m
  • Antenna asymmetry
